# InternalArcStart Property (IRichLineStyle)

Gets or sets whether to join the starts of the two line elements closest to the outer ones with an arc when there are four or more lines defining the [RichLine](Interop.dsAutomation~Interop.dsAutomation.IRichLine.html) entity.

### Property Value

True to join the starts of the two line elements closest to the outer ones with an arc when there are four or more lines defining the RichLine entity, false to not
