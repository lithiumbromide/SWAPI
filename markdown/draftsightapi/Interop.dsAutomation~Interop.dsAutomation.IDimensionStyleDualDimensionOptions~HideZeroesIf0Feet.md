# HideZeroesIf0Feet Property (IDimensionStyleDualDimensionOptions)

Gets or sets whether to hide or show zero feet (0') for dual Dimensions when the [format](Interop.dsAutomation~Interop.dsAutomation.IDimensionStyleDualDimensionOptions~Format.html) of the dual Dimension is set to Architectural, Architectural Stacked, or Engineering.
