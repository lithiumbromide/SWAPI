# HideZeroesIf0Inches Property (IDimensionStyleLinearDimensionOptions)

Gets or sets whether to hide or show zero inches (0") for linear Dimensions when the [format](Interop.dsAutomation~Interop.dsAutomation.IDimensionStyleDualDimensionOptions~Format.html) of the dual Dimension is set to Architectural or Engineering.
